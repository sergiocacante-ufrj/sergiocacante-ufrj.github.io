## Reproducible workflow

1. Review point coordinates and spatial reference systems.
2. Explore distributions, missing values, outliers and clustering.
3. Document variogram assumptions, method choice, cross-validation and spatial uncertainty.
4. Publish map products with dataset permissions and appropriate coordinate precision.
