## Research question

How can the static monotonic response of a torpedo anchor embedded in clay be reproduced and compared across analytical and finite element approaches?

## Current methodological focus

1. Define and document the benchmark geometry, strength profiles, loading direction and modelling assumptions.
2. Establish independent analytical / numerical reference results.
3. Extract model load–displacement histories and verify the force and displacement definitions.
4. Assess interface assumptions and mesh sensitivity **after** a reproducible baseline comparison.

**Baseline study context:** idealized anchor length 17.2 m, diameter 1.10 m; undrained strength model \(s_u=kz\), with illustrative gradients 1.5, 3 and 6 kPa/m. These parameters are *benchmark context*, not design recommendations.
