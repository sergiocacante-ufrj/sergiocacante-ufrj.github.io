## What the notebook does

- Declares depth and three **illustrative** \(s_u\) gradients.
- Computes \(s_u(z)=kz\) with stated units.
- Draws a multi-profile chart and a small summary table.

No capacity, pullout force, constitutive behavior or PLAXIS result is computed. These examples serve as a traceable input parameter visualization, not a calibrated design model.
