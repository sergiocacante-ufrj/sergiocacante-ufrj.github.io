## Consulting areas

### Geotechnical design

- Foundations (shallow and deep), settlement and deformation.
- Ground improvement and analysis of soft soils.
- Excavations, slope stability, retaining systems and soil–structure interaction.
- Finite element analysis with project-appropriate model calibration.

### Hazard and risk assessments

- Seismic hazard and site effects.
- Mass movement and slope instability.
- Geotechnical inputs for environmental impact assessments.

### Integrated civil engineering

The existing website presents **Geocerto** as a consultancy in geotechnical, structural and hydraulic engineering for Colombia. The new site keeps professional services separate from academic research and educational tools.
